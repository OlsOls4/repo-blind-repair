# Release notes — dataset v3 + repo state, 20 Nov 2026

## What this release is

  (20,000 records), at
  https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olsols4/repo-blind-repair-synthetic-corpus-20k

## The Day P result, in context

Day P — a faithful clone of the public Patchsmith E010/V6 recipe
(Apache 2.0), 31B, inference-only — scored Public Score **0.15**
(SUCCEEDED, checked 20 Nov 2026 18:00–18:01 GMT), the first nonzero
of 15 scored dailies. 0.15 is below the 0.24 leaderboard top: it
joins the block, it does not lead. The corpus did not produce this
score: Day P involved no training on it. Nothing here shows
synthetic training transferring to real bugs.
